MHT CET 2026

Maharashtra Health and Technical Common Entrance Test · conducted by State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra

CAP Round 1 liveEngineeringState — MaharashtraTwo sessions a year; the better percentile counts

CAP Round 1 live. Final merit list out 27 July 2026. CAP Round 1 option entry open 28–30 July 2026.

1days to go

CAP Round 1 option entry for MHT CET 2026 is open from 28 to 30 July 2026. Fill and lock your choice list carefully — a badly ordered list costs more seats than a low percentile does.

MHT CET is the state-level test that feeds Maharashtra's Centralised Admission Process. The PCM group leads to BE/BTech and BPharm; the PCB group leads to pharmacy and agriculture programmes. Admission is entirely through CAP, run by DTE Maharashtra — there is no college-level walk-in for CAP seats. From 2026 the CET Cell applies a best-of-two-attempts rule.

Mode: Computer-based test Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
Passed Class 12 with Physics and Mathematics compulsory for PCM, plus Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology or a technical vocational subject.
Minimum marks
45% aggregate in the relevant subjects for open category, 40% for reserved categories in Maharashtra.
Domicile
CAP seats under state quota require Maharashtra domicile and the associated candidature type. Non-Maharashtra candidates may sit the exam but usually compete under All India seats using JEE Main.
Age
No upper age limit.
Attempts
No limit. From 2026 you may sit both sessions and the higher percentile is used automatically.

Available in English, Marathi and Urdu.

What you get after clearing it

BE / BTech

Four years at one of 350+ engineering colleges. Placement quality varies enormously — COEP, VJTI, ICT and the top autonomous colleges are in a different league from the tail.

BPharm and PharmD

Pharmaceutical manufacturing, clinical research and regulatory affairs. Maharashtra has a dense pharma industry around Mumbai and Pune.

BSc Agriculture

State agriculture universities, agri-business, and state agriculture department roles.

Frequently asked questions

Which session score is used?

From 2026, the CET Cell automatically takes the higher percentile of your two attempts for the merit list. You do not need to request it.

Is there negative marking?

No. Attempt every question.

Can I get a CAP seat with a JEE Main score?

Yes. Maharashtra reserves a share of seats for JEE Main scorers, and non-Maharashtra candidates typically compete for All India seats that way.

How much does the choice list order matter?

A great deal. Allotment runs strictly down your preference list, so putting a reach college below a safe one can cost you the reach seat entirely.
