IBPS Clerk 2026

Common Recruitment Process for Customer Service Associates · conducted by Institute of Banking Personnel Selection

Prelims in OctoberBankingNationalOnce a year

Prelims in October. Prelims scheduled for 10 and 11 October 2026, mains on 27 December 2026.

73days to go

The Customer Service Associate recruitment, still universally called IBPS Clerk, is the entry-level clerical cadre role at eleven public sector banks. It is the one major banking exam with no interview: your mains score alone decides selection. Postings are state-specific, so you apply for one state and compete only against candidates for that state.

Mode: Online prelims and mains. No interview. Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
A bachelor's degree in any discipline.
Age
20 to 28 years. Standard category relaxations apply.
Language
You must be proficient in the official language of the state or union territory you apply for — reading, writing and speaking. This is verified before appointment.
State choice
You may apply for only one state or union territory, and you compete only within it.

Prelims: 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes with sectional timing. Qualifying only.

Mains: 190 questions, 200 marks, 160 minutes.

Negative marking is 0.25 per wrong answer at both stages.

There is no interview. The mains score is the entire basis of selection.

Syllabus

Scope

The same four areas as IBPS PO, pitched slightly easier but with tighter time pressure.

Numerical Ability

Simplification, number series, arithmetic word problems, and basic data interpretation.

Reasoning

Puzzles, seating arrangement, syllogism, inequality, coding-decoding, blood relations and direction sense.

English

Reading comprehension, error spotting, fillers, cloze test and para jumbles.

Financial Awareness

Banking basics, RBI, government schemes, budget, and current affairs of the past six months.

Cutoff trends

Cutoffs are published state by state and differ widely — a mark that clears in one state may fall well short in another.

Prelims (out of 100)Around 60 – 78 depending on state

Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Delhi and Uttar Pradesh usually see the highest cutoffs.

What you get after clearing it

Customer Service Associate

Branch counter operations, account services, cash handling and customer support. Basic pay under the current settlement starts around ₹24,050, with gross monthly pay near ₹40,000 depending on location.

Promotion

Clerk to Officer Scale I through internal promotion or the JAIIB and CAIIB professional exams, which also carry pay increments.

Work-life balance

Fixed branch hours and a stable posting within your chosen state make this one of the more predictable government-sector jobs.

Full pay scales and posting details for every exam are in the careers and salary guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is there really no interview?

Correct. IBPS Clerk selection is based purely on the mains score.

Can I apply for more than one state?

No. One state or union territory only, and your language proficiency for it will be verified.

How fast is promotion to officer?

It varies by bank, but clearing JAIIB and CAIIB materially speeds it up and adds increments in the meantime.
