IBPS PO 2026

Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers / Management Trainees · conducted by Institute of Banking Personnel Selection

Prelims next monthBankingNationalOnce a year

Prelims next month. Notification out 1 July 2026. Prelims on 22 and 23 August 2026.

24days to go

IBPS reopened the application window for two days on 29 and 30 July 2026. Vacancies were revised upward from 6,715 to 7,365.

IBPS PO is the common recruitment for Probationary Officers and Management Trainees at eleven participating public sector banks. Three stages: a sectional-timed prelims, a mains with a descriptive paper, and an interview. Final selection weighs mains and interview at 80:20 — prelims marks are dropped entirely.

Mode: Online prelims and mains, plus an interview Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university, with the degree in hand by the date specified in the notification.
Age
20 to 30 years. Relaxations: 3 years OBC, 5 years SC/ST, 10 years PwBD, 5 years for ex-servicemen.
Computer literacy
Operating and working knowledge of computers is required, evidenced by a certificate or by having studied computing at school or college.
Language
Proficiency in the official language of the state you apply for is preferred and is checked at the interview stage.
Attempts
No limit.

Prelims total: 100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes with sectional timing. It is qualifying only.

Mains total: 155 objective questions for 200 marks, plus 25 marks of descriptive writing.

Negative marking is 0.25 marks per wrong answer in both prelims and mains.

Final merit is 80% mains and 20% interview. Prelims marks are discarded once you qualify.

Syllabus

Quantitative Aptitude and Data Analysis

Simplification and approximation · Number series · Quadratic equations · Data interpretation in tables, bars, lines, pies and caselets · Arithmetic word problems · Data sufficiency

Reasoning

Puzzles and seating arrangements — the largest block · Syllogism · Inequality · Blood relations · Direction sense · Order and ranking · Input-output · Coding-decoding · Data sufficiency · Critical reasoning

English

Reading comprehension · Cloze test · Error detection · Sentence rearrangement · Fillers · Word usage and vocabulary · Paragraph completion

General and Banking Awareness

Banking terms and abbreviations · RBI functions and monetary policy · Types of accounts and NPAs · Financial markets · Government schemes · Budget and Economic Survey · Current affairs of the past six months

Computer Aptitude

Computer fundamentals · Operating systems · MS Office · Internet and networking · Database basics · Security and shortcuts

What you get after clearing it

Probationary Officer — JMGS-I

Two-year probation, then confirmation as an officer. Basic pay under the current bipartite settlement starts around ₹48,480, with gross monthly pay in the region of ₹85,000 depending on location and allowances.

Promotion path

Scale I to Scale II (Manager) to Scale III (Senior Manager) and upward. Promotion is faster than in most government services, and internal exams accelerate it further.

Postings

Anywhere across the bank's network. Expect rural or semi-urban branches early in your career.

Exit options

Banking experience feeds into NBFCs, fintech credit teams, insurance and financial services roles.

Full pay scales and posting details for every exam are in the careers and salary guide.

Frequently asked questions

Do prelims marks count towards the final merit?

No. Once you clear prelims, those marks are discarded. Final selection is 80% mains and 20% interview.

How is IBPS PO different from SBI PO?

SBI runs its own separate recruitment with its own notification, pattern and higher pay. IBPS PO covers eleven other public sector banks under one common process.

Which section decides the outcome?

Reasoning puzzles and data interpretation. They carry the most marks and take the most time, and speed on them is what separates candidates.

Is there a bond or service commitment?

Most participating banks require a service bond. Read the notification for the bank you are allotted.
