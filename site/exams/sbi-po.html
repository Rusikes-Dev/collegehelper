SBI PO 2026

State Bank of India — Probationary Officer · conducted by State Bank of India

Applications closedBankingNationalOnce a year

Applications closed. Notification out 18 June 2026 for 1,500 posts. Applications closed 8 July 2026.

SBI recruits its Probationary Officers separately from the IBPS common process. The pattern is close to IBPS PO but harder, particularly the mains, which includes a data analysis section that is notoriously time-pressured. The 2026 cycle offers 1,500 posts. Final selection weighs mains at 75% and the group exercise plus interview at 25%.

Mode: Online prelims and mains, then a group exercise and interview Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
A bachelor's degree in any discipline. Final-year students may apply provided they produce proof of graduation by the specified date.
Age
21 to 30 years. Standard relaxations apply.
Attempts
General and EWS candidates get four attempts, OBC seven, SC/ST unlimited within the age limit.

Prelims is qualifying only.

Mains objective is 200 marks plus 50 marks descriptive, for 250 total.

Final merit: 75% mains, 25% group exercise and interview.

The Data Analysis section is the hardest part of any mainstream banking exam.

Syllabus

Scope

Same four areas as IBPS PO, but the data analysis and reasoning questions are consistently harder and the descriptive paper carries double the weight.

Data Analysis

Complex caselets, missing-data DI, arithmetic DI and data sufficiency.

Descriptive

One formal or informal letter and one essay, usually on a banking, economic or social topic.

What you get after clearing it

Probationary Officer at SBI

Two-year probation covering branch banking, credit and operations. Pay and perquisites are the highest among public sector banks.

Fast-track roles

Strong performers move into credit, treasury, international banking and the bank's analytics functions.

Scale

SBI's size means more specialised roles and more internal mobility than at smaller public sector banks.

Frequently asked questions

Is SBI part of IBPS?

No. SBI conducts its own recruitment with its own notification and pattern.

How many attempts do I get?

Four for General and EWS, seven for OBC, unlimited for SC/ST within the age limit.

Is the descriptive paper evaluated for everyone?

Only for candidates who clear the objective mains cutoff.
