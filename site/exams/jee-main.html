JEE Main 2026

Joint Entrance Examination (Main) · conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA)

2026 cycle completeEngineeringNationalTwice a year — January and April sessions

2026 cycle complete. JoSAA counselling underway · JEE Main 2027 notification expected October–November 2026

2days to go

JEE Main is India's largest engineering entrance exam. NTA runs it in two sessions each year and the better of your two scores is used for the final rank. Paper 1 leads to BE/BTech seats through JoSAA counselling; Papers 2A and 2B lead to BArch and BPlan. Clearing the cutoff percentile also makes you eligible to sit JEE Advanced for the IITs.

Mode: Computer-based test (Drawing section of Paper 2A is pen-and-paper) Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
Passed Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Candidates who passed in 2025 or 2026, or who appear in 2027, are eligible for JEE Main 2027.
Age limit
No age limit set by NTA. Individual institutes may apply their own criteria.
Minimum marks
None to sit the exam. But for admission to NITs, IIITs and GFTIs you need 75% in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST) or be in the top 20 percentile of your board.
Attempts
No cap on the number of years. Both sessions in a year count as one attempt cycle.
Subject rule
Paper 1 needs PCM. Paper 2A (BArch) needs Physics, Chemistry and Maths. Paper 2B (BPlan) needs Mathematics.

Marking for Paper 1: +4 for a correct MCQ, −1 for a wrong MCQ, 0 for unattempted.

All five numerical-value questions per subject are compulsory and carry no negative marking.

Scores are normalised into percentiles across shifts, so your raw marks and your percentile are not the same thing.

From the 2026 January session onward NTA uses facial biometric authentication at exam centres.

What you get after clearing it

BTech at an NIT/IIIT/GFTI

Core engineering, software, analytics and consulting roles. Campus placements at top NITs regularly cross ₹15–25 LPA for computer science branches.

Route to JEE Advanced

Clearing the percentile cutoff and being in the top 2,50,000 gets you a shot at the 23 IITs.

State and private admissions

Many state and private universities accept JEE Main scores, including under Maharashtra's CAP process.

Frequently asked questions

Which session score counts?

The better of your two normalised scores. If you appear in both January and April, NTA takes the higher percentile for the final rank list.

Is there negative marking in the numerical questions?

No. All five numerical-value questions per subject are compulsory and carry no negative marking. Only MCQs carry −1.

Do I need 75% in Class 12 to write JEE Main?

No. You can write the exam regardless. The 75% (65% for SC/ST) rule applies only at the time of admission to NITs, IIITs and GFTIs.

How many times can I attempt JEE Main?

There is no attempt limit on the exam itself, though the top engineering institutes have their own age and year restrictions for admission.
