CLAT 2026

Common Law Admission Test · conducted by Consortium of National Law Universities

Registration opens 3 AugustLawNationalOnce a year, in December

Registration opens 3 August. CLAT 2027 notification out 22 July 2026. Registration 3 August to 31 October 2026, exam 6 December 2026.

5days to go

CLAT 2027 will be held on Sunday 6 December 2026 from 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm. The Consortium has confirmed there is no change to the syllabus or exam pattern. An Expert Committee report on the exam's format is open for public feedback until 31 August 2026, but any resulting changes would apply from CLAT 2028.

CLAT is the common entrance test for the five-year integrated law programmes and the LLM programmes at 27 National Law Universities. The UG paper is entirely comprehension-driven — every section gives you a passage and asks you to reason from it, so reading speed matters more than memorised legal knowledge. Note that NLU Delhi runs its own separate test, AILET.

Mode: Offline, pen and paper, single shift Official site ↗

Eligibility

UG qualification
Passed or appearing in Class 12 with at least 45% marks, or 40% for SC and ST candidates.
PG qualification
An LLB or equivalent with at least 50% marks, or 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates.
Age
No upper age limit for either programme.
Attempts
No limit.
Results awaited
Candidates awaiting Class 12 results may apply, but must produce the qualifying certificate at admission.

Total: 120 questions, 120 marks, 120 minutes.

Marking: +1 for a correct answer, −0.25 for a wrong answer.

Every section is passage-based. You are given the legal principle or the factual context and must reason from it — no prior legal study is assumed.

The paper is offline, on OMR. Practise the transfer of answers under time pressure.

Syllabus

English Language

Comprehension passages of 450 words drawn from contemporary and historical writing, tested on inference, main idea, tone, argument and vocabulary in context.

Current Affairs and GK

News-based passages covering national and international events, arts and culture, historical events of continuing significance, and current affairs from roughly the past twelve months.

Legal Reasoning

Passages on legal matters, public policy or moral philosophy. You apply the stated rules to new facts. No prior knowledge of law is required.

Logical Reasoning

Short argumentative passages tested on premise and conclusion identification, analogies, assumptions, inference and argument evaluation.

Quantitative Techniques

Short data sets — graphs, tables or text — tested on ratios, percentages, averages, mensuration and basic algebra at Class 10 level.

What you get after clearing it

Corporate law firm practice

Tier-one firms recruit heavily from the top NLUs, with day-zero offers in the ₹16–20 LPA range.

Litigation

Chamber practice under a senior advocate, then independent practice. Slower to pay off but the route to the bar and eventually the bench.

Judicial services

State judiciary exams after the LLB, leading to appointment as a Civil Judge.

In-house counsel

Legal teams at companies, banks and technology firms.

Policy and academia

Think tanks, legislative research, LLM programmes and teaching.

Full pay scales and posting details for every exam are in the careers and salary guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is NLU Delhi covered by CLAT?

No. NLU Delhi conducts its own entrance test, AILET, with a separate application and schedule.

Do I need to know law already?

No. Legal reasoning passages give you the principle and ask you to apply it. Prior legal study confers little advantage.

Is the syllabus changing for 2027?

No. The Consortium has confirmed the UG and PG syllabus and pattern are unchanged. The Expert Committee report under review would affect CLAT 2028 at the earliest.

How many NLUs participate?

27 National Law Universities use CLAT for admission.
