JEE Advanced 2026

Joint Entrance Examination (Advanced) · conducted by One of the seven zonal IITs, on rotation

2026 exam completeEngineeringNationalOnce a year, usually in May

2026 exam complete. JoSAA 2026 counselling underway

291days to go

JEE Advanced is written by roughly 2.5 lakh candidates who clear the JEE Main percentile cutoff. Both papers are compulsory and the question style is markedly harder than JEE Main — multi-concept problems, multiple-correct options and matching-type questions dominate. Seats are allotted through JoSAA alongside the NITs and IIITs.

Mode: Computer-based test, two compulsory papers Official site ↗

Eligibility

JEE Main rank
Be among the top 2,50,000 successful candidates in JEE Main Paper 1, category-wise.
Age
Born on or after 1 October 2001. Five-year relaxation for SC, ST and PwD candidates.
Attempts
A maximum of two attempts, in consecutive years.
Class 12
Must have appeared in Class 12 (or equivalent) for the first time in the current or previous year.
Prior admission
You must not have accepted an IIT seat in an earlier year, regardless of whether you continued.

Exam pattern and marking

PaperSubjectsDurationNote
Paper 1Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics3 hoursCompulsory
Paper 2Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics3 hoursCompulsory

The number of questions, marks and marking scheme are not fixed in advance — the organising IIT decides them each year and announces them in the paper itself.

Expect single-correct MCQs, multiple-correct MCQs with partial marking, numerical answer type and matching-list questions.

Both papers must be attempted. Missing one means your candidature is not considered.

AAT (Architecture Aptitude Test) is a separate test for BArch at IIT Kharagpur and IIT Roorkee.

Syllabus

Scope

Broadly the same topic list as JEE Main, but tested at much greater depth. Expect problems that chain three or four concepts together in a single question.

Physics emphasis

Mechanics, electrodynamics, modern physics and thermodynamics carry the heaviest weight.

Chemistry emphasis

Physical chemistry numericals, organic reaction mechanisms and inorganic qualitative analysis.

Mathematics emphasis

Calculus, coordinate geometry, algebra and probability.

What you get after clearing it

BTech at an IIT

Median packages at older IITs sit in the ₹18–25 LPA range, with computer science branches higher.

Research and higher study

IIT degrees are the standard feeder into MS and PhD programmes in India and abroad.

Dual degree and integrated MTech

Five-year programmes at IITs that save a year over a separate masters.

Frequently asked questions

Can I skip Paper 1 and write only Paper 2?

No. Both papers are compulsory and skipping either one disqualifies you.

Does the JEE Main score carry into JEE Advanced?

No. JEE Main only decides whether you are eligible. Your IIT rank comes purely from JEE Advanced marks.

Is the marking scheme announced in advance?

Not usually. The organising IIT prints the scheme in the paper. Read the instructions carefully in the first few minutes.
