SSC CHSL 2026

Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination · conducted by Staff Selection Commission

Notification expectedSSCNationalOnce a year

Notification expected. SSC CHSL 2026 notification expected in the second half of 2026.

CHSL recruits Lower Division Clerks, Junior Secretariat Assistants and Data Entry Operators across central ministries and departments. It is the most accessible central government recruitment for anyone who has finished Class 12, and the syllabus overlaps heavily with SSC CGL — many candidates prepare for both together.

Mode: Computer-based, two tiers plus a typing or skill test Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
Passed Class 12 or equivalent. For Data Entry Operator in the Comptroller and Auditor General's office, Science stream with Mathematics is required.
Age
18 to 27 years. Three years relaxation for OBC, five for SC/ST, ten for PwBD.
Typing
LDC and JSA posts require a typing test — 35 words per minute in English or 30 in Hindi.
Data entry speed
DEO posts require 8,000 key depressions per hour on a data entry skill test.

The typing and data entry tests are qualifying, not scored, but failing them ends your candidature.

Syllabus

Scope

The same four subjects as SSC CGL but pitched at Class 10 to 12 level rather than graduate level.

Quantitative Aptitude

Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry and data interpretation at Class 10 standard.

English

Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms and correct usage.

General Intelligence

Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, series, analogy, coding-decoding, classification, and figural patterns.

General Awareness

India and its neighbours, history, culture, geography, economy, polity, science and current events.

What you get after clearing it

Lower Division Clerk / Junior Secretariat Assistant

Pay Level 2, roughly ₹19,900 – ₹63,200. Clerical and record work in ministries and attached offices.

Data Entry Operator

Pay Level 4 or 5, roughly ₹25,500 – ₹81,100 for the higher grades. Better paid than LDC and available in CAG and other departments.

Promotion path

LDC to UDC to Assistant, with departmental exams available to speed this up.

Full pay scales and posting details for every exam are in the careers and salary guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is CHSL easier than CGL?

The syllabus is pitched lower, but with far more applicants per post the competition is not soft. Cutoffs regularly sit above 150 out of 200.

Can I prepare for CGL and CHSL together?

Yes, and most serious candidates do. The subjects are identical; only the depth differs.

Does the typing test carry marks?

No, it is purely qualifying — but you must clear it to be appointed.
