GATE 2026

Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering · conducted by IISc Bangalore and the seven older IITs, on rotation

Registration opens 14 AugustEngineeringNationalOnce a year, across weekends in February

Registration opens 14 August. GATE 2027 registration opens 14 August 2026. Exam across six days in February 2027.

16days to go

GATE tests a full undergraduate engineering or science curriculum in a single three-hour paper. The score serves two purposes: admission to MTech and MS programmes at IITs, IISc, NITs and other institutes with a monthly stipend, and recruitment at public sector undertakings including IOCL, NTPC, BHEL, GAIL and ONGC. The score stays valid for three years.

Mode: Computer-based test Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
Currently in the third year or higher of any undergraduate degree, or already holding a degree in engineering, technology, architecture, science, commerce or arts.
Age
No age limit.
Attempts
No limit.
Papers
You may appear in up to two papers from the permitted combinations.

Exam pattern and marking

ComponentMarksDetail
General Aptitude15Verbal and numerical aptitude, common to all papers
Subject / Engineering Mathematics85Paper-specific
Total10065 questions, 3 hours

Question types: multiple choice, multiple select, and numerical answer type.

MCQs carry negative marking — one-third of the marks for 1-mark questions and two-thirds for 2-mark questions.

Multiple select and numerical answer questions carry no negative marking.

Thirty papers are offered. The score is valid for three years from the date of the result.

Syllabus

Structure

Each of the 30 papers has its own syllabus, published on the official GATE site. General Aptitude is common to all.

General Aptitude

Verbal aptitude — grammar, vocabulary, reading and narrative sequencing. Quantitative aptitude — data interpretation, numerical computation, mensuration and elementary statistics. Analytical and spatial aptitude.

Engineering Mathematics

Linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, complex variables, probability and statistics, and numerical methods. Present in most engineering papers.

Popular papers

Computer Science and Information Technology (CS), Mechanical (ME), Electrical (EE), Electronics and Communication (EC), Civil (CE), Instrumentation (IN), Chemical (CH), Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DA).

Cutoff trends

Qualifying marks are far lower than the score you need for an IIT MTech seat or a PSU shortlist.

TargetIndicative requirement
Qualifying (General, CS)Around 25 – 30 out of 100
IIT MTech admissionScore above 700 – 800 on the GATE scale, i.e. AIR in the low hundreds
NIT MTech admissionGATE score of roughly 450 – 650
PSU recruitmentUsually top 1–2% of the paper — varies sharply by PSU and discipline

PSUs publish their own cutoffs after each recruitment notification and these vary by company and by year.

What you get after clearing it

MTech or MS at an IIT or IISc

Two years with an MHRD assistantship — currently ₹12,400 per month for GATE-qualified candidates.

PSU recruitment

Engineer or Management Trainee roles at IOCL, NTPC, BHEL, GAIL, ONGC, PowerGrid and others. Entry CTC typically ₹12–18 LPA.

Research

A route into PhD programmes and into national laboratories such as BARC, DRDO and ISRO.

Teaching

A GATE score plus an MTech is the standard entry into assistant professor roles at engineering colleges.

Full pay scales and posting details for every exam are in the careers and salary guide.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a GATE score valid?

Three years from the date the result is announced.

Can I appear in two papers?

Yes, in one cycle you may sit up to two papers, but only from the permitted combinations listed in the brochure.

Do all PSUs recruit through GATE?

Most major ones do, but each publishes its own separate notification with its own cutoff and selection process. Qualifying GATE does not automatically put you in any PSU pool.

Is negative marking applied to numerical answer questions?

No. Only multiple choice questions carry negative marking.
