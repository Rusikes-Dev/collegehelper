CUET UG 2026

Common University Entrance Test (Undergraduate) · conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA)

University counselling underwayUniversityNationalOnce a year

University counselling underway. CUET UG 2026 result out 23 June 2026. Participating universities are running their own counselling rounds.

CUET UG replaced board-marks cutoffs at central universities. You pick up to five subjects matched to the courses you want, sit a computer-based test in May or June, and then apply separately to each university's own counselling process. Delhi University runs CSAS, BHU and other universities run their own portals — the test score alone does not get you a seat.

Mode: Computer-based test across multiple phases Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
Passed or appearing in Class 12 or an equivalent examination.
Age
No age limit set by NTA. Individual universities may apply their own criteria.
Subject choice
Up to five subjects, which must match what your target courses require. Check each university's prospectus before locking your choices.
Attempts
No limit, but the exam runs only once a year.

Exam pattern and marking

ComponentQuestionsTimeMarking
Each chosen subject50, all compulsory60 minutes+5 correct, −1 wrong
General Test5060 minutes+5 correct, −1 wrong

You may select up to five subjects in total, drawn from 37 available options.

Domain subjects follow the NCERT Class 12 syllabus.

The General Test covers general knowledge, current affairs, reasoning, numerical ability and logical reasoning.

Scores are normalised across shifts where a subject runs in more than one session.

Syllabus

Domain subjects

Based on the NCERT Class 12 syllabus for each subject. There are 37 to choose from, covering sciences, commerce, humanities, languages and vocational areas.

General Test

General knowledge · Current affairs · General mental ability · Numerical ability · Quantitative reasoning at Class 8 level · Logical and analytical reasoning

Languages

13 languages available as a main language paper, plus 20 more as additional language options.

Cutoff trends

NTA does not publish a CUET cutoff. Each participating university releases its own course-wise and category-wise cutoff after the result.

Because cutoffs are set by each university and not by NTA, check the specific university's admission portal rather than any aggregated list.

What you get after clearing it

Undergraduate degrees at central universities

BA, BCom, BSc, BBA and integrated programmes at Delhi University, BHU, JNU, AMU, Jamia and more than 250 participating institutions.

Onward paths

A central university degree feeds into CAT for management, UPSC and state PSC preparation, CUET PG, and postgraduate study abroad.

Frequently asked questions

Does a good CUET score guarantee admission?

No. You must separately register for each university's counselling — DU's CSAS, BHU's portal and so on. The score is only the qualifying input.

How many subjects should I take?

Up to five. Choose based on what your target courses actually require, not on what you find easiest — a subject your course does not accept is wasted effort.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. Each correct answer is +5 and each wrong answer is −1.

Is CUET held more than once a year?

No. CUET UG runs once annually, usually in May and June.
