UPSC CSE 2026

Civil Services Examination · conducted by Union Public Service Commission

Mains in 3 weeksCivil ServicesNationalOnce a year

Mains in 3 weeks. Prelims 2026 result out 15 June. Mains 2026 begins 21 August.

23days to go

UPSC CSE Mains 2026 runs on 21, 22, 23, 29 and 30 August 2026, in two sessions each day — 9:00 am to 12:00 noon and 2:30 pm to 5:30 pm.

The Civil Services Examination recruits for 21 services including the IAS, IPS, IFS and IRS. Three stages spread over about a year: a screening Prelims in May, a nine-paper Mains in August, and an interview early the following year. Prelims marks do not count towards the final merit — only Mains and the interview do.

Mode: Prelims objective, Mains descriptive, then a personality test Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
A bachelor's degree from a recognised university, in any discipline. Final-year students may sit Prelims but must produce proof of graduation before Mains.
Age
21 to 32 years for General. Relaxations: 35 for OBC, 37 for SC/ST, 42 for PwBD.
Attempts
Six for General and EWS, nine for OBC, unlimited for SC/ST until the age limit. PwBD candidates get nine.
Nationality
Indian citizens for IAS, IPS and IFS. Certain other services also admit specified categories of Nepali, Bhutanese and Tibetan-refugee candidates.
Physical standards
Apply to IPS and some other services, checked at the medical examination stage.

Prelims marks are used only for screening. They do not count towards the final merit list.

Merit total is 2025 marks: 1750 from the seven counted Mains papers plus 275 from the interview.

Prelims negative marking is one-third of the marks for each wrong answer.

Papers A and B are qualifying only. Failing them means the rest of your Mains papers are not evaluated.

One from 48 options — 26 literature papers plus subjects like Geography, PSIR, Sociology, Anthropology, History, Public Administration and several engineering and science disciplines.

What you get after clearing it

IAS — Indian Administrative Service

District administration, then state and central secretariat postings. Entry pay Level 10, basic ₹56,100. A Cabinet Secretary draws ₹2,50,000 basic.

IPS — Indian Police Service

ASP on probation, then SP of a district. Same entry pay level as the IAS.

IFS — Indian Foreign Service

Diplomatic postings at Indian missions abroad, with a foreign allowance that substantially raises take-home pay.

IRS — Income Tax and Customs

Assistant Commissioner on entry. Postings concentrated in metros and major commercial centres.

Other Group A services

Indian Audit and Accounts Service, Indian Railway Management Service, Indian Information Service, Indian Postal Service and others.

Full pay scales and posting details for every exam are in the careers and salary guide.

Frequently asked questions

Do Prelims marks count in the final merit?

No. Prelims is purely a screening test. Your final rank comes from the 1750 Mains marks plus 275 interview marks.

How do I choose an optional subject?

Pick on the basis of your genuine interest, availability of material and mentors, and overlap with GS papers. Chasing a supposedly 'scoring' optional you dislike is the more common mistake.

Is CSAT counted?

No, it is qualifying only. You need 33%, which is 66 out of 200. But candidates from non-maths backgrounds do fail it, so do not ignore it.

How long does one full cycle take?

About twelve to fourteen months from notification to final result.
