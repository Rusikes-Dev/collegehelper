CAT 2026

Common Admission Test · conducted by Indian Institutes of Management, on rotation — IIM Indore for 2026

Registration opens 3 AugustManagementNationalOnce a year

Registration opens 3 August. Notification released 25 July 2026. Registration runs 3 August to 15 September 2026.

5days to go

CAT 2026 will be conducted by IIM Indore on Sunday 29 November 2026. Registration opens 3 August at 10:00 am and closes 15 September at 5:00 pm. The fee has risen to ₹2,700 for General and ₹1,350 for reserved categories.

CAT is India's principal MBA entrance test. Scores are accepted by all 22 IIMs and more than 1,300 other business schools including FMS Delhi, and the management departments at IIT Bombay and IIT Delhi. The exam is a single two-hour paper split into three strictly timed 40-minute sections that you cannot move between.

Mode: Computer-based test in three slots on a single day Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
A bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks, or 45% for SC, ST and PwD candidates.
Final-year students
Eligible to apply, subject to producing the degree at the time of admission.
Age
No age limit.
Attempts
No limit on the number of attempts.
Professional qualifications
CA, CS and ICWA holders may apply on the basis of those qualifications.

Marking: +3 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong MCQ. Type-in-the-answer questions carry no negative marking.

You cannot move between sections. Each section locks after its 40 minutes.

Results are reported as scaled scores and percentiles, normalised across the three slots.

Conducted across roughly 170 test cities.

Syllabus

Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension

Four to five reading comprehension passages · Para jumbles · Para summary · Odd sentence out · Sentence completion. There is no formal grammar or vocabulary section in the current pattern.

Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning

Tables, bar and line graphs, caselets · Arrangements and matching · Venn diagrams · Games and tournaments · Binary logic · Quantitative reasoning sets

Quantitative Ability

Arithmetic — percentages, ratios, time and work, time speed distance, mixtures, profit and loss · Algebra — equations, inequalities, functions, logarithms · Geometry and mensuration · Number systems · Modern maths — permutations, combinations, probability, sequences

Cutoff trends

IIMs shortlist on percentile, but the final call depends on academic profile, work experience, gender and academic diversity, and the interview.

Institute tierIndicative percentile for a General category call
IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Calcutta99 – 99.5+
IIM Lucknow, Kozhikode, Indore98 – 99
Newer IIMs90 – 96
FMS Delhi, SPJIMR, MDI96 – 99
Good non-IIM B-schools80 – 90

Percentile is only the shortlist filter. Composite scores at the older IIMs weight Class 10 and 12 marks, graduation marks and work experience heavily.

What you get after clearing it

MBA from a top IIM

Consulting, investment banking, product management, general management. Average packages at the older IIMs sit in the ₹28–35 LPA range.

MBA from a newer IIM or good private school

Sales and marketing, operations, analytics, banking and financial services roles, typically ₹12–20 LPA.

Beyond placements

The alumni network and the two-year signalling value matter as much as the first job for most candidates.

Frequently asked questions

Who is conducting CAT 2026?

IIM Indore, on 29 November 2026.

Can I switch between sections?

No. Each section is locked to its own 40 minutes and you must attempt them in the given order.

Does a 99 percentile guarantee an IIM seat?

No. The older IIMs build a composite score that also weights your Class 10 and 12 marks, graduation percentage, gender and academic diversity, and work experience.

How much preparation time do I need?

Six to eight months of consistent work is typical for a working professional. Full-time candidates often do it in four to five.
