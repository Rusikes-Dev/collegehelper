SSC CGL 2026

Combined Graduate Level Examination · conducted by Staff Selection Commission

Tier 1 next monthSSCNationalOnce a year

Tier 1 next month. Applications closed. Tier 1 scheduled for August–September 2026.

17days to go

Three changes this cycle: sectional timing of 15 minutes per section in Tier 1, the return of Assistant Audit Officer and Assistant Accounts Officer posts, and a new Paper 3 in Tier 2 for AAO/AAAO candidates only.

SSC CGL fills Group B and Group C posts across central government ministries and departments — Income Tax Inspector, CBI Sub-Inspector, Assistant Section Officer, Excise Inspector, Assistant Audit Officer and more than forty others. Two computer-based tiers decide the outcome; Tier 1 only qualifies you for Tier 2 and does not count towards the final merit.

Mode: Computer-based, two tiers Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
A bachelor's degree in any discipline. Assistant Audit Officer and Assistant Accounts Officer prefer or require CA, CS, MBA, Cost Accountancy or a Masters in Commerce or Economics.
Statistical posts
Junior Statistical Officer needs a degree with Mathematics at Class 12 with at least 60%, or a degree with Statistics as a subject.
Age
18 to 32 depending on the post. Most posts sit in the 18–30 or 20–30 band. Standard relaxations apply — 3 years OBC, 5 years SC/ST, 10 years PwBD.
Nationality
Indian citizen, or a Nepali or Bhutanese subject, or specified categories of persons of Indian origin.
Registration
One Time Registration on the new SSC portal is mandatory. Old OTR IDs do not carry over.

Tier 1 total: 100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes, with negative marking of 0.50 per wrong answer.

New in 2026: sectional timing. Once a section's 15 minutes are up you cannot return to it.

Tier 1 is qualifying. The final merit is built from Tier 2 alone.

Tier 2 negative marking is 1 mark per wrong answer in Paper 1 Session I, and 0.50 in the Computer Knowledge module.

What you get after clearing it

Assistant Audit Officer / Assistant Accounts Officer

Group B gazetted. Pay Level 8, scale ₹47,600 – ₹1,51,100. The highest-paying and most sought post in CGL.

Income Tax Inspector

Group C, Pay Level 7. Assessment and investigation work under CBDT, with good promotion prospects to Income Tax Officer.

Assistant Section Officer, CSS

Group B, Pay Level 7. Ministry postings in Delhi with the clearest path to Section Officer and Under Secretary.

Sub-Inspector, CBI

Group B, Pay Level 7. Investigation work, transferable, with a field allowance.

Inspector, Central Excise and GST

Group B, Pay Level 7. Enforcement and assessment under CBIC.

Frequently asked questions

Does Tier 1 count in the final merit?

No. Tier 1 only decides who advances. Your final rank depends entirely on Tier 2.

What does sectional timing change?

Everything about your strategy. You can no longer bank time from an easy section to spend on a hard one. Practise each section in a fixed 15-minute block.

Which post should I put first in my preference list?

That depends on whether you value pay, promotion speed, posting location or work-life balance. AAO pays best; ASO in a ministry gives Delhi postings; Income Tax Inspector offers strong promotion prospects.

Can final-year students apply?

Yes, provided you can produce your degree by the cut-off date specified in the notification.
