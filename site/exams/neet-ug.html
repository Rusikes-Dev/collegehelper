NEET UG 2026

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate) · conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA)

Result out · counselling nextMedicalNationalOnce a year

Result out · counselling next. Re-NEET result declared 16 July 2026. MCC counselling schedule awaited at mcc.nic.in.

7days to go

The NEET UG 2026 paper scheduled for 3 May was cancelled after a paper leak. A re-examination was held on 21 June 2026 and the result was declared on 16 July 2026. About 11.21 lakh of roughly 20 lakh candidates qualified. MCC has not yet published the counselling schedule.

NEET UG is the only route to an MBBS or BDS seat anywhere in India, government or private. Around 20 lakh candidates sit it each year for roughly 1.1 lakh MBBS seats. Counselling splits in two: the Medical Counselling Committee handles the 15% All India Quota plus AIIMS, JIPMER, deemed and central universities, while each state runs its own counselling for the remaining 85%.

Mode: Pen-and-paper, OMR sheet Official site ↗

Eligibility

Qualification
Class 12 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology and English as a compulsory subject.
Minimum marks
50% aggregate in PCB for General, 40% for SC/ST/OBC, 45% for General-PwD.
Age
At least 17 years as on 31 December of the year of admission. No upper age limit.
Attempts
No limit.
Nationality
Indian nationals, NRIs, OCIs, PIOs and foreign nationals may all appear, under the applicable seat categories.

Frequently asked questions

What happened to the 3 May 2026 exam?

It was cancelled after a paper leak. NTA held a re-examination on 21 June 2026 and declared the result on 16 July 2026.

Does qualifying NEET guarantee a seat?

No. Qualifying only makes you eligible for counselling. Whether you get a seat depends on your rank, category, quota and the seat matrix.

Do I register separately for counselling?

Yes. Your NEET application does not carry over. You must register fresh on the MCC portal for All India Quota, and on your state portal for state quota.

What is the difference between AIQ and state quota?

All India Quota is 15% of government college seats, open to any NEET-qualified Indian candidate regardless of home state. The remaining 85% is state quota, which almost always requires domicile proof.
