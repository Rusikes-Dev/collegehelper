About

College Helper is an independent site that tracks Indian competitive exams — the dates, the syllabus, the eligibility rules, the cutoffs, and what actually happens after you clear one.

Why it exists

Exam information in India is scattered across dozens of official portals that publish PDFs with no consistent format, and aggregator sites that bury the answer under advertising. A student trying to work out whether their MHT CET merit number reaches a particular college, or when the UPSC Mains actually starts, should not have to open eleven tabs.

Where the data comes from

  • Exam dates, patterns and eligibility come from the official notifications published by NTA, UPSC, SSC, IBPS, the IIMs, the Consortium of NLUs, the Maharashtra State CET Cell and others.
  • The MHT CET predictor runs on the official MHT CET 2025 CAP Rounds I, II and III cut-off lists published by the Maharashtra State CET Cell.
  • The NEET predictor runs on Medical Counselling Committee All India Quota allotment lists for Rounds 1 to 3.

What this site does not do

  • It does not sell coaching, courses or counselling services.
  • The exam pages are free and need no account. The two college predictors are one-time paid unlocks — that payment funds the site, so there are no ads and no selling of leads.
  • Your rank or merit number is never uploaded. Even in the paid predictors, the calculation happens in your browser once the data is unlocked.

Corrections

Schedules change and mistakes happen. If something here is wrong or out of date, tell us and it will be corrected. Include the exam and a link to the official notice where you can.

Please verify before you act. College Helper compiles dates, syllabus and cutoff information from official notifications and established education sources. Schedules change, notices get revised, and errors are possible. Always confirm anything that affects a deadline, a fee payment or a counselling choice against the official website of the conducting body before acting on it.
